### What's New
- **Background Notification Delivery:** Receive custom school, stream, and class announcements even when the app is closed.
- **Hero A/L Exam Countdown:** Opening the app spotlights your days left for GCE A/L before smoothly animating into the header chip.
- **Structured Subject Buckets & Class Lists:** Choose A/L subjects from Stream → Bucket lists and pick your class directly from your Grade + Stream list (with custom entry fallback).
- **Auto A/L Exam Year by Grade:** Picking Grade 12 automatically selects 2028 A/L and Grade 13 selects 2026 A/L (still manually changeable).
- **Targeted Multi-Event Calendar Updates:** Support for multiple school events and teaching advisories per calendar day tailored to specific audiences.
- **In-App Feedback Resolution Alerts:** Students get an in-app banner when their submitted bug report or suggestion has been addressed.
- **Post-Update What's New & Feedback Welcome:** See highlights after every update and easily jump to the feedback card in Settings.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed custom announcements triggering duplicate device notifications every time the app is opened.
- Fixed the Settings **Test Popup** button so it opens properly on weekends as well.
- Fixed release versioning so updated APKs accurately report their installed version in About & Update Checker.
- Streamlined the pulsing live community counter and added smoother UI animations throughout.
